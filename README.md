## About the Project

**Uncannily Normal** is a first-person psychological horror experience set in a quiet suburban neighborhood that never quite feels right. You wake up at 5:00 AM. The store is open. The streets are empty. The fog is thick. And something is watching.

The game leans into low-poly, PSX-inspired visuals, heavy atmosphere, and slow-building tension rather than jump scares. Explore the neighborhood, interact with your surroundings, and try to figure out why everything feels… uncanny.

## Why I Made This

I wanted to take what I learned from my previous 3-day prototype and push it further. This time I gave myself a full week to focus on atmosphere, lighting, fog, and that specific uncomfortable feeling that something is wrong even when nothing is happening.

The goal was to create a small but polished horror experience that feels deliberate and unsettling.
